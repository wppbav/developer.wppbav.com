AURA, our AI brand research, is now available on the BAV MCP server. AURA studies measure how AI models perceive brands,
using the same models as our consumer research.

<!-- truncate -->

Seven new tools give agents access to AURA data:

- `get-aura-studies` and `get-aura-audiences` to find the available studies and audiences.
- `get-aura-pillar-data`, `get-aura-powergrid-data`, `get-aura-personality-data` and `get-aura-imagery-data` for the
  core brand models.
- `get-aura-drift-data` to compare how AI sees a brand against how consumers see it.

The tools are available to users with access to AURA. See the
[BAV MCP Server](/docs/2.x/ai/mcp/bav#aura-tools) documentation for details.
