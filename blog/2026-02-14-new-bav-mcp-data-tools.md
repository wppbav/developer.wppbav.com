The BAV MCP server can now answer a much wider range of brand questions. We've added six new data tools, bringing
more of the analyses you know from the platform to your agents.

<!-- truncate -->

- **Commitment Funnel** (`get-commitment-funnel-data`) shows how consumers move from awareness to preference and
  loyalty.
- **Perception SWOT** (`get-perception-swot-data`) highlights a brand's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and
  threats.
- **Category Drivers** (`get-category-drivers-data`) shows which attributes drive preference in a category.
- **Archetypes** (`get-archetypes-data`) maps brands to their archetypes.
- **Partnership Exchange** (`get-partnership-exchange-data`) finds brands with complementary imagery for partnerships.
- **Toplist** (`get-toplist-data`) ranks the top brands in a market on a given metric.

Agents can also create brand collections with the new `create-collection` tool, making it easy to save a set of
brands for later analysis.

See the [BAV MCP Server](/docs/2.x/ai/mcp/bav) documentation for the full list of tools and their parameters.
